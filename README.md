This project presents the design and simulation of a complete 8-channel Wavelength Division Multiplexing (WDM) optical communication system using OptiSystem simulation software. The main objective of the project was to analyze the performance of different optical transmission and reception techniques in high-speed long-distance optical fiber communication systems.

The system was designed to transmit multiple optical channels simultaneously over a single optical fiber by assigning a unique wavelength to each channel. Different optical source configurations were implemented and compared, including Continuous Wave (CW) Laser, Ideal Single Mode Laser, and Directly Modulated Laser, in order to investigate their influence on transmission quality, signal stability, and overall system performance.

At the transmitter side, Mach-Zehnder Modulators (MZM) were used to externally modulate the optical carriers with electrical data signals before combining all wavelengths using a WDM Multiplexer. The multiplexed optical signal was then transmitted through a long-distance optical fiber link consisting of Single Mode Fiber (SMF), Dispersion Compensation Fiber (DCF), and Erbium-Doped Fiber Amplifier (EDFA) stages. The DCF section was integrated to reduce chromatic dispersion effects, while the EDFA was used to compensate for attenuation and maintain acceptable signal power levels over extended transmission distances.

At the receiver side, the optical channels were separated using a WDM Demultiplexer and detected using different photodetection techniques, including PIN Photodiodes and Avalanche Photodiodes (APD). A detailed comparison between both receiver architectures was performed to evaluate receiver sensitivity, noise performance, and signal recovery efficiency.

Comprehensive performance analysis was carried out for all WDM channels through Bit Error Rate (BER) analysis, eye diagram evaluation, optical power measurements, Q-factor analysis, and signal quality assessment. The project also investigated the impact of laser characteristics, fiber impairments, receiver sensitivity, and amplification stages on the overall communication system performance.

This project provided practical understanding of modern optical communication systems, WDM network architectures, optical modulation techniques, dispersion management, optical amplification, and performance optimization in high-capacity fiber optic networks.
